## PARDON — The Barbarian from Iron Bolt

Welcome to an ancient fantasy world of gods, necromancers, legendary artifacts, strange creatures, and heroes with even stranger ambitions.

**PARDON — The Barbarian from Iron Bolt** follows a powerful barbarian drawn into a rescue mission after a royal daughter is kidnapped by an immortal necromancer. An eccentric mage recruits Pardon, introduces him to his team, and becomes his unlikely companion on the journey ahead.

Together, they face undead armies, bizarre magic, dangerous monsters, philosophical conversations around the campfire, and increasingly absurd battles.

Set within the larger Trashalaxy universe, this irreverent fantasy adventure blends heroic quests, RPG logic, and dark comedy in a world that never takes itself too seriously.

## The Trashalaxy universe

Trashalaxy is a universe where fantasy meets science fiction. Its stories explore a shared world through different characters and adventures.

Dark humor is a defining part of Trashalaxy’s voice. Epic events collide with absurdity; characters speak boldly and without restraint; and blunt language, including profanity, helps shape their personalities and the rhythm of the stories. We admire the freedom of humor in *Rick and Morty*, *South Park*, and *Jay and Silent Bob*: the ability to play with genre conventions while still telling a compelling story.
